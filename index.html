Prelim documents

Concept Paper
Title, introduction, background, general and specific problems, objectives that mirror the problems, proposed system description, scope and delimitations.
Work Breakdown Structure
Tasks selected from the provided WBS for all six phases, with descriptions written for your project and a correct count per phase.
Budget Plan
All six phases costed by month, personnel and material rates shown, phase totals and overall total filled in and adding up, price references.

Midterm documents

System Specifications
Purpose and scope match your concept paper; functional specs (FS-01, FS-02…); hardware, sensor and software specs fit your actual system.
Gantt Chart
WBS tasks with duration, start and finish dates, predecessors, assigned members, and shaded bars across the weeks.
Wireframe
Purpose, objectives, target users, wireframe summary, functional requirements, design standards, and each screen with a sketch, components and user actions.

Peer evaluation

Each groupmate rated you from 1 to 5 on five questions:

  1. Effort on assigned tasks
  2. Reliability in meeting deadlines and responsibilities
  3. Quality of contributions to the group's work
  4. Initiative in helping the group succeed
  5. Overall value of your contribution

Your weighted mean is the average of every rating you received from all your groupmates.

Prelim grade

Prelim = Documentation × 80% + Peer evaluation % × 20%

Peer evaluation % = weighted mean ÷ 5 × 100. A weighted mean of 4.50 is 90%.
